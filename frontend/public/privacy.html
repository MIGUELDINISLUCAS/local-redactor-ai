Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Your messages are anonymised on your own computer. The text you write, the documents you load, and the link between placeholders and your real details are never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

How your messages are handled

The extension holds your message before it reaches ChatGPT or Claude, detects sensitive details using an AI model running locally on your machine, and lets you review exactly what will be sent. Only the anonymised version is sent to the AI provider. The reply is restored to your real details on your device — with one exception, described under Connectors and app widgets below.

  • Your original text never leaves your device.
  • The detection model runs offline on your machine — no text is ever sent anywhere to be analysed. The model itself is downloaded once, the first time the app runs (see below).
  • Placeholder mappings live in your browser's session storage and are cleared when the tab closes.
  • Saved rules and preferences stay in your browser's local storage.

Connectors and app widgets

ChatGPT and Claude can display interactive cards from connected apps — an email composer, a calendar entry, a file from a connected drive. These are rendered in a separate frame that the extension is not able to read or change.

Inside such a card, placeholders are not replaced with your real details, and anything the card sends is sent by the provider's own servers rather than by your browser — so the extension can neither anonymise nor block it. Sending from one of these cards is the same as sending directly from the provider.

This limit is deliberate rather than an oversight. Restoring your real details inside a card that is about to transmit them would hand the provider exactly the information you are protecting. The extension shows a warning while such a card is on screen — check for any remaining placeholder text, for example [PERSON_001], before you send from one.

When the app does contact a server

The app is offline by default. It reaches the network only in these moments — never to send your content:

WhenWhat is sentWhere
First run — downloading the detection model Nothing about you: a plain download request for the model file (about 1.6 GB, fetched once and then reused offline) github.com
Activating a licence Your licence key and an anonymous machine identifier (a one-way hash of your computer's name, user name, operating system and processor type) localredactor.xyz
Renewing a subscription The same two values, re-sent shortly before the licence expires so it can be extended localredactor.xyz

No message content, document text, detected values or placeholder mappings are included in any of these requests. Aside from them, the app makes no outbound calls at all. The desktop app also checks for updates.

Licences and payment

Payments are handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Your subscription record lives with Stripe; we keep no customer database of our own.

Your licence key contains an 8-character one-way hash of your email address — not the address itself — along with the type of licence and its order reference. The licence stored on your computer additionally names the machine it was activated on, so a key cannot be used on unlimited computers.

Diagnostics

There is no automatic error reporting. If you ask for help, the extension can assemble a diagnostic report describing software versions, whether the engine is running, and how many rules you have saved. It contains none of your messages, rules or personal data, and it is never transmitted automatically — it is placed on your clipboard or into an email draft, and only you decide whether to send it.

What we never do

  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking
  • No cookies
  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No transmission of your messages, documents or detected values
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties
